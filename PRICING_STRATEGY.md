# LogIntelligence - Pricing Strategy & Feature Breakdown

## Philosophy

**Free Tier Goal**: Provide enough value for individuals and small projects to get real utility, while creating natural upgrade paths for production/professional use.

**Pro Tier Goal**: Remove limitations and add professional features that matter for teams, scale, and mission-critical applications.

## Pricing Justification

### Why These Limits?

**1,000 errors/month (Free)**
- Typical small project generates 10-50 errors/day
- 1,000/month = ~33 errors/day
- Perfect for side projects, development, testing
- Encourages upgrade for production use

**100 AI credits/month (Free)**
- ~3 AI analyses per day
- Enough to understand error types
- Encourages thoughtful use of AI
- Natural upgrade path for production monitoring

**7-day retention (Free)**
- Sufficient for active bug fixing
- Reduces storage costs
- Encourages upgrade for historical analysis

**$29/month (Pro)**
- Competitive with similar tools (Sentry: $26+, Rollbar: $39+)
- Covers AI API costs + infrastructure
- Affordable for indie developers and small teams
- Scales profitably

### Free → Pro Conversion Signals

- Banner when 80% of AI credits used
